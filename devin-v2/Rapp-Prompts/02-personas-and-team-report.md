# 02 · Persona summaries, resources, team report

Paste everything below the line into Devin. Requires prompt 01.

---

**Goal:** Give every attendee a persona-based summary with resources to share, and let me report back to my team.

## Pause point 1 · Before building (READ-DO)
1. Define 6–8 personas (e.g. executive buyer, technologist, practitioner, partner, recruiter, founder, chief of staff). Each one needs a label, a one-line gist, how to approach them, 3 resources to share and a follow-up template. **(must: no blank fields)**
2. Auto-assign a persona from role/title keywords, and let me override it with one tap.

## Pause point 2 · Build (READ-DO)
3. Person view: one summary sentence ("Ana is an executive buyer: ..."), approach tips, resources, and a copyable follow-up with the name and topic filled in.
4. Team report: people grouped by persona with key notes, follow-ups owed, sessions attended, takeaways and my top 3 opportunities.
5. Export: copy, download `.md`, email (mailto) and print to PDF.

## Pause point 3 · Before saying "done" (DO-CONFIRM)
- [ ] Every persona has all five fields filled in.
- [ ] Report headings read naturally: no doubled plurals or broken grammar. **(must)**
- [ ] The downloaded `.md` opens. The mailto body is trimmed to about 1,800 characters so mail apps don't cut it off.
- [ ] Reassigning a persona immediately updates the summary, resources and template.
- [ ] Screenshots of a person view and the report.
