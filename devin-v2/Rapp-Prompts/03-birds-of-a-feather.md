# 03 · Birds-of-a-feather sessions

Paste everything below the line into Devin. Requires prompt 01.

---

**Goal:** Run birds-of-a-feather (BoF) sessions: propose topics, vote, schedule, and capture what was said.

## Pause point 1 · Build (READ-DO)
1. Add a session type `bof` with topic, host, attendees (linked to people), discussion notes and takeaways.
2. Topic board: propose a topic, vote (one vote per topic per person), sorted by votes.
3. "Schedule it": turn a topic into a BoF in a free agenda slot that doesn't conflict with anything. **(must)**
4. BoF notes and takeaways flow into the team report.
5. Seed 2 example BoFs and 4 proposed topics.

## Pause point 2 · Before saying "done" (DO-CONFIRM)
- [ ] Proposed a topic, voted, scheduled it, and it appears on the agenda without a conflict.
- [ ] Attendees link to their person pages, and those pages list the BoF.
- [ ] Takeaways show up in the team report.
- [ ] Zero console errors. Screenshot of the board and of a BoF.
