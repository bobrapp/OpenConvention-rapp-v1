# 07 · Connection web, matching, small groups

Paste everything below the line into Devin. Requires prompts 01–02.

---

**Goal:** Build a subject web of connections that matches me with individuals and builds small groups.

## Pause point 1 · Build (READ-DO)
1. Subject web: an SVG graph linking topics to people. Tapping a node filters to it.
2. "Who should I meet": rank people by overlap with my interests and goals, and show the reason in plain words.
3. Intro suggestions: pairs of people I've met who should meet each other, with a copyable intro message.
4. Small-group builder: pick a topic, get 3–6 people, and schedule them as a BoF in a free slot.

## Pause point 2 · Before saying "done" (DO-CONFIRM)
- [ ] With 15+ people, graph labels don't overlap or get clipped (split long labels, scale the font). **(must)**
- [ ] Every match shows a human-readable reason.
- [ ] Scheduling a group creates an agenda item with no conflict.
- [ ] Screenshot of the web and of the match list.
